# Project notes for Claude

This is Devanshi Sharma's (UI/UX designer, Noida) portfolio site. It's static HTML/CSS/JS with no framework and no build step. It's live on GitHub Pages at https://devanshi9193-cloud.github.io/devanshi-portfolio/ (repo `devanshi9193-cloud/devanshi-portfolio`, `main` branch, root folder).

## Structure (Nov 2026 redesign, at her request)
- No header. A frosted-glass pill nav (`.dock.pill` in `index.html`) at the bottom of every page: text links Work (with her avatar), About, Playground, Journal; the current page sits in a white pill; then a divider and a solid sky-blue "Resume ↗" pill (darker on hover). It replaced the macOS icon dock (`dock()` in `app.js` is now a no-op).
- Four top pages (`PAGES` in `app.js`, routes `#work` (default), `#about`, `#playground`, `#journal`), plus the two detail page kinds (case studies, journal posts).
- Work: big serif line "Devanshi Sharma believes good design should feel effortless.", Currently / Previously, then simple project cards (image + "one-line description / kind · year"; no project names like Jasper Colin or Runway on the cards). Currently line says "Delhi NCR, India".
- Journal: the 4 entries as tiles shaped like the work cards. A post page (`renderPost`) uses the same frame as the case study pages (`fx-*`): rule with "← Journal" and category · read time, big title, meta grid (Type, Reading time, Written by, Written: Over coffee) beside the deck, a tinted full-width cover, then ruled rows (each `h` block starts a row, its text in `.jp-text`), and "Next entry" in the `fx-next` style. Don't use a `<header>` element in pages: an old global rule makes it sticky.
- About: intro, Skills & tools, Workshops & speaking, Recommendations ("Things I like" removed for now; she will add more later), then photos of her in groups with a heading over each (no numbers).
- Playground: 9-piece jigsaws (`jigsaw` in `app.js`) of her paintings and her two quote cards, and a Notes wall. Notes are shared through her Supabase project: `NOTES_DB` (url + publishable key) at the top of `app.js`; table and rules in `supabase/notes.sql`. Visitors can only add notes and read approved ones; she approves a note by ticking `approved` in the Supabase Table Editor. The sender sees their own note as "Waiting for approval" (kept in their browser). Max 3 notes per visitor per day. The line under the wall reads "Thank you for your kind words!". If `NOTES_DB` is empty, notes stay in the visitor's browser.
- Footer: her yellow-flowers photo (`assets/images/footer-flowers.webp`) pinned to the bottom at full width, its blue sky continuing above and fading into the page (`.ft.field`). The footer is pulled up (negative margin) so the fade starts right under the last content, with `#app` stacked above it so that content stays clickable; text sits in the sky, with name + line, Pages / Contact / Elsewhere / Local time columns and the bottom line. 
- Fonts (don't change them): every heading (h1–h6, footer column headings, quotes) is Libre Baskerville, upright, never italic; body TWK Lausanne (licensed, not in the repo; Figtree shows until `assets/fonts/twk-lausanne-*.woff2` are added); small labels Barlow Condensed.
- Colours (Nov 2026, matched to her footer flowers photo): primary is the photo's sky blue (`--royal` #0B5470, deep #0A3D52, soft #DCEBF1); accent is the flower yellow (`--red` token, #E2BC69, soft #F8EDD3); stem green #4F5F46 (`--sage`). Old matcha/kraft tokens point at these. Off-white paper, navy ink. Every heading stays the same navy ink (white only in the footer); never colour a heading or part of one. Work card hover: no lift or zoom; the image gets a translucent sky-blue frosted-glass layer (blurred) with "View case study →" in a glass pill (`.wc-hover`). Journal cards get the same glass hover with "Read entry →"; journal tiles alternate soft blue and soft yellow.
- Motion and cursor (Nov 2026, at her request): page titles (`.wk-h`, `.pg-h`, `.fx-title`) play in on load; sections, cards, photos, footer columns rise in on scroll (selectors in `REVEAL` in `app.js`, styles in the "SCROLL ANIMATION" block of `desk.css`), and card images settle from a slight zoom. The cursor is a sky-blue dot (bigger over links and buttons; text cursor in fields; mouse only). The favicon and `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` are a large sky-blue dot (#0B5470).
- Look: `desk.css` (loaded after `styles.css`) holds the theme. No stars, tape, doodles, tilts or notebook paper.

## Where things live
- Content data is at the top of `app.js`: `PROJECTS`, `CASES`, `SCREENS`, `SKILLS`, `WALL` (sketchbook wall), `POSTS`, `TESTIMONIALS`, `WORDS`.
- Her personal photos are in `assets/images/` (`hero-selfie.jpg`, `life-*.jpg`). Resize new photos to about 1000px (hero 1400px) before adding them.
- Routing: `route()` in `app.js` (hash based). Home section anchors are `#work`, `#about`, `#journal`, `#contact`.
- `styles.css` is layered: base styles, then later blocks override them (blue scrapbook theme, layout layer, then per-section blocks at the end). Add new overrides at the end.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. All animation loops check `reduce`.
- Scroll reveal: anything with `.reveal` (plus the selectors in `REVEAL` in `app.js`) fades and rises in when it scrolls into view, using the CSS `translate`/`scale` properties. Never put `.reveal` on draggable items (`.gw-it`, `.dy-it`), because dragging also uses `translate`. The hero plays in on page load instead.
